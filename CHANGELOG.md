# **Changelog**

All notable changes to Skales will be documented in this file.

The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/),

and this project adheres to [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

## v12.9.55 - Mango

**A release about work you can hand over: documents that look designed, a Friend Mode that follows through, a calmer chat, and every cent of your AI spending in one view.**

Word, PowerPoint, Excel and PDF files now come out on real templates and slide masters in your brand kit, with charts, diagrams, contents with page numbers and your own fonts, and Skales checks the file before you get it. Every chat has its working folder and a Files tab, discarded work goes to the trash, and agents can browse in their own sandbox browser. Friend Mode follows up on one open point at a time, listens to "done" or "later" and asks on one channel only. Azure OpenAI and Google Vertex AI join the providers, web search falls back instead of giving up, and a private SearXNG is one click away.

The chat is calmer, with no avatars at the bubbles, a side panel that remembers whether you want it open and a header that lines up with Code. The Costs view and the year heatmap count every answer you paid for, the dashboard shows your week and editable widgets, notifications have four tones, and personal data such as an IBAN can be held back before it leaves, now also for pictures and Code. WhatsApp attachments arrive again under their own names, DeepSeek through OpenRouter no longer loops, a second Skales no longer takes the port of the first, and Google Vertex AI answers.

### Fixed

- **Tooltips on the top row are whole again.** The hints of the incognito, Iris and composer buttons open below the button like every other tooltip, instead of rising under the window's title strip and being cut off.
- **The start screen speaks plainly while macOS asks for the keychain.** The note sits centred under the tagline in two short lines, in your language, and so does the setup line after an install.
- **Every built-in skill ships with the files it refers to.** The architecture review skill finds its report template again.
- **The side panel never says "No model call yet" next to a cost.** An older chat that has answered but kept no per-call record shows its cost without that line.
- **Cards no longer slide under the open side panel.** Approval cards, the plan, helper and team cards, the language-check questions and the slash menu make room for the panel like the conversation does.
- **Bubbles and cards stand on one line.** Messages, approval and docked cards and the composer share the same left and right edges at every width.
- **The session list opens where you asked for it.** It hangs under the "⋮" menu instead of somewhere in the chat.
- **One quiet shadow for every bubble.** Sent and received messages carry the same small shadow, and nothing cuts it off.
- **The sidebar is closed at the top in Flat and Classic.** Its head carries the same thin line as the other edges.
- **The memory cloud shows what your memories are about.** Filler words such as "this", "nicht" or "aber" and verbs and adjectives such as "braucht" or "wichtig" are left out in every app language and on the phone, also style words a setting writes capitalised such as "Playful" or "Bold", the app's own upkeep records no longer appear as topics, and the cloud wears your accent colour.
- **The session count matches the list beside it.** Sessions counts the same chats that "Jump back in" lists, without briefings, channel chats or chats nobody wrote in.
- **Full-screen previews clear the window buttons.** The preview's head starts below the macOS traffic lights and beside the Windows caption buttons, its close button always takes the click, and Escape closes it even while you work inside the page.
- **Save as image saves the page.** The picture shows the whole page as a browser shows it, top to bottom, whatever was scrolled into view, the zoom or the side panel.
- **Asked to delete, Skales deletes.** Removing a task you asked for no longer runs in circles: the task list Skales reads first now counts as having seen the task.
- **Free memory is measured right on a Mac.** The system status and the local model checks count the memory macOS can hand out, not only its empty pool that read 0.0 GB, and scripts written for "python3" or "nodejs" run.
- **A calendar that needs signing in again says so.** When Google ends the calendar connection the widget says it in one sentence and offers Reconnect instead of Google's raw error.
- **Skales Code opens in your colours.** The window's first frame already wears your accent instead of flashing the default green first.
- **The Cockpit count sits at the edge.** The number on the Cockpit row lines up with the notice dots on the right.
- **The dashboard Tasks card leaves the archive out.** Archived housekeeping runs no longer fill the Tasks card or the Iris task list, so they show the same tasks as the Tasks page and the Cockpit count, and the AI Brain row shows the whole model name on hover instead of cutting it off.
- **Google Vertex AI answers.** Requests to Vertex AI now go to the chat address Google documents for its OpenAI-compatible endpoint; before, a version segment was added that Vertex does not have, so chat, Lio, skills and Visuals with Vertex got no answer. Azure OpenAI and Vertex AI also get the longer answer time thinking models need, and their own two-letter marks in Settings.
- **Web search through OpenRouter is sent like every other OpenRouter call.** When the models may search too, the OpenRouter search now names Skales and its categories, so it reaches the models OpenRouter only serves to agents.
- **The way to the trash is named right.** The working-folder hints in the chat and Skales' own directions now say Settings > Security & Privacy > Trash; before, they pointed at a Trash category that does not exist.
- **Letter paper on Word reports shows the address, and a header keeps the page numbers.** A DIN 5008 letter made with create_docx now shows its address block and references on the first page; before, the report's own page setting hid that first-page header. A letterhead header no longer removes the report's footer with its page numbers, and page numbers alone keep the report's running title at the top. A letterhead part always replaces the report's own, never adds a second one.
- **The safety note names your own browser.** Settings > Safety now lists using your own signed-in browser for an agent set to the sandbox among the steps that ask in every mode, as the approval card already did.
- **YouTube transcripts are read again.** Reading a YouTube link brought back the title but no transcript, because YouTube now hands its caption files to the website only with a token a browser makes. Skales now asks the way YouTube's own app does and gets the words. When there really is none, it says whether the video has no captions or YouTube wanted a sign-in.
- **Files sent over WhatsApp keep their names.** A document someone sends you on WhatsApp is now saved under its own name in files/documents, like a file you attach in the chat, instead of a number. A second file with the same name is saved beside the first as name-2. Photos and voice notes, which have no name, are named as before.
- **Open in the Files list opens the file in its own app.** In the desktop app, Open now starts the file with the program your computer uses for it (Word, Preview, Excel), also for files outside the Skales folder. Scripts and programs are shown in their folder instead of being run. In a browser it opens in a new tab as before.
- **An error Skales does not know reads as a sentence, not as code.** When a provider fails in a way Skales does not recognise, the chat now says which provider stopped and that the error is unknown; the provider's own text is under "Show error details". The Test button in Settings still shows that text right away.
- **A branded conversation keeps the brand's voice in its answers too.** Once a document in a chat uses a Brand Kit, or the chat's project has one, the assistant writes its replies and drafts in that kit's tone and leaves out what the kit says to avoid, not only in the files it makes.
- **Your documents are found by meaning, not only by their words.** Searching the documents you added to Skales now also compares meaning through your embeddings, so a question about fruit finds the page about apples. A new document gets its vectors on the next search, and they go when the document goes. Without embeddings the search works by words as before.
- **Asked for slides or a spreadsheet, the assistant makes the file.** On the computer it now knows from the start that it can write a PowerPoint deck on slide masters, an Excel sheet, and read the Brand Kit, as on the phone, instead of offering an HTML page to print.
- **Skales names the search service it really uses.** With a search provider chosen but no key for it, the assistant said it searched with that provider while another one answered. It now names the one that answers first.
- **Searching a time span no longer costs a full re-index.** A conversation search limited to a period ("what did we talk about yesterday") threw away the meaning vectors of all other conversations, so the next ordinary search paid to make them again. They now stay.
- **The Files tab knows what helpers made.** Files written by a background helper that a chat dispatched now show up in that chat's Files tab. File cards in the Code window follow a file you took over or moved, like in the chat. A conversation deleted for good no longer leaves its file list behind; one in the bin keeps it for a restore.
- **DeepSeek through OpenRouter answers instead of looping.** OpenRouter could send DeepSeek requests to a host whose copy wrote filler into its thinking until the output budget was gone, with no answer. Skales now asks OpenRouter to skip that host for DeepSeek models; all other hosts and models are routed as before.
- **A new install on OpenRouter starts on Claude Sonnet 5.5.** Setup picks it when OpenRouter lists it, and the model list carries it. Existing installs keep the model they use.
- **A second Skales no longer lands on the port of the one already running.** Starting another copy beside a running app could pick the same port on macOS, so pages could open in the wrong instance. A port now counts as taken as soon as anything answers on it.
- **Holding back personal data is easy to find and covers pictures and Code.** The switch that holds a message with an IBAN, a card number or a key before it goes to a cloud model is now also in Settings > Security & Privacy, next to Safety Mode, and it is still off until you turn it on. A held message now waits in the composer, so sending it anyway is one key. A picture with a caption and a line typed in a Code window (also from the phone, an edited message, or a queued line sent now) are held the same way, once.
- **Skales starts faster after an install or update, and the window stays responsive.** The local server no longer waits for the check of the program files; that check now runs after start without blocking the window, and its result is kept, so later starts skip it. On Windows and Linux, unpacking the program files after an update shows its progress on the start screen instead of a frozen window, and "localhost" answers at once instead of after a two-second detour.
- **"This is taking longer than usual" only when it really is.** The start screen stays up while Skales is unpacking files or waiting for the macOS keychain, and says so (on macOS: choose "Always Allow" if it asks, the dialog may be behind other windows). The slow-start message with Restart and Quit only appears when the local server has been silent for a real while, and Restart is no longer the main button while Skales is still working.
- **Own windows wear your colours from the first frame.** The Iris name animation starts in your colour instead of black, and the Code window follows your accent like the rest of the app instead of staying green.
- **Window buttons and headers on one line.** In the Code window the macOS window buttons now sit on the header's centre line with clear room before the menu button, at every Skales size; on Windows the window buttons take the header's height.
- **Discover can be looked at without joining.** The join and the one-time character step have a visible way back (and Escape), and their colours follow your accent instead of a pink or orange tint.
- **A new chat shows up once in the sidebar.** Starting a chat from the phone browser, with a code folder or by just sending could list the same conversation two or three times until the next reload. Every way in now updates the one entry.
- **Attached text, code and HTML files are real files.** A file you attach in chat, in the browser on another device or from the phone app is saved in your workspace under files/documents, next to documents that arrive on WhatsApp, and the message names where it is. Asking to render, export or send it now works from that file instead of the assistant searching for a copy, and a long file that does not fit into the message is still there in full.
- **WhatsApp that signs in but never finishes starting restarts itself.** After an app restart WhatsApp could stay signed in without ever becoming ready. Skales now restarts it on the saved login, at most twice, and the assistant calls this "linked, still connecting" instead of "not set up".
- **A switched-off Ollama stays off.** With Ollama or LM Studio switched off in Settings, nothing uses it anymore: not as a fallback, not for scheduled jobs, routing or composer suggestions, even while an Ollama server runs on the machine. A small local model no longer delays the start of an answer. With routing on, the waiting line says that a routing model reads your message first.
- **"Download backup" works in the installed app again.** A packaging rule meant for leftover backup files also removed the backup download route from every installer, so the download in the browser and from another device, and the automatic backup before an update, failed. The rule now matches only real backup files, and the build checks that every route reaches the installer.
- **Background jobs never answer with raw tool-call markup.** When a model wrote a tool call as text instead of running it, a scheduled job, a helper or the final summary of a multi-helper job could show pages of that markup as its answer, or finish with a check mark and nothing in it. Every job answer is now cleaned the way chat answers are, a call written as text is named instead of shown, and an answer with nothing left counts as failed with the reason. A spelling of these calls with a space in the tag, which DeepSeek models use, is now recognised in chat as well, so the call runs instead of being dropped.
- **The final summary of a multi-helper job can check the helpers' work.** It now has read-only tools (read files, list folders, search) to compare a claim with what is on disk, and nothing that writes, runs code or goes online.
- **Images, voice messages and documents sent to you on WhatsApp arrive again.** After a WhatsApp Web update, received files could no longer be downloaded and the assistant said the image had not arrived. When WhatsApp Web's own download fails, Skales now fetches the file from WhatsApp and decrypts it itself. Photos (also HD), voice messages, videos, PDFs and HTML documents are saved in your workspace under the message's own name, and the assistant gets exactly that file.
- **No stand-in for a file that did not load.** When a received file really cannot be loaded, the assistant says so and asks for it again; it no longer reaches for another image from your uploads and claims to have seen it.
- **A WhatsApp Web build pin that did not take is named.** WhatsApp can serve its current build even when an older one is pinned. Settings now says so instead of asking for a restart that would change nothing.
- **Identity maintenance stays one quiet job.** The nightly run no longer splits itself into helpers and a summary with a report in the chat; it does its steps in one run, says nothing when it works and sends one notice with the reason when it fails.

### Changed

- **The chat head sorts itself.** The model choice sits on the right beside the side panel button, and Memory Mode is a button next to it again: one click switches between Always Remember, Compact and Minimal. "Start incognito chat" is in the "⋮" menu under handing the conversation to Iris.
- **Cockpit is there from the start.** Whatever you answered in setup, Cockpit is on and stands in the Home sidebar right under the fixed rows. Unpin it and it waits under More; pin it again from there. From the chat sidebar it opens as a page, the same one the Home sidebar opens.
- **"Needs you" shows what it counts.** Click the number on the Cockpit row or on the page and the board shows exactly those cards. Failed runs of the app's own housekeeping, such as identity maintenance, are archived instead of piling up: off the board and the count, kept under a new Archived filter in Tasks.
- **A calmer sidebar head.** Home and Chat are two centred icons with their names as tooltips, and the Cockpit's Schedule and Tasks entries are marked by their text alone, without a filled background.
- **The side panel shows everything at once.** On a window wide enough for the chat beside it the panel is open the first time, on a narrower one it waits behind its button; close or open it and that choice is remembered. Its overview no longer folds: on top agent, model, context, cost and runtime, with every model call of the session as a small heat strip, below it environment, what the context holds, background tasks, connectors and changes. The plan stays pinned in the conversation while it runs, and Background tasks in the "⋮" menu lands on its block.
- **Arrange the whole dashboard.** Everything under the briefing bar in Calm, and under Live in Power, is one list you can sort: drag a card or a widget onto another, or use the arrows. Each view keeps its own order. A quiet "Edit layout" button on the right starts it, and widgets wear their own icons instead of emojis.
- **Heatmaps that say what they show.** "Daily activity, last 12 months" fills its whole row, with empty dots for the days before and after, and "Your active hours" spreads its 24 hours across the whole card in the same round dots, with the hours written underneath.
- **Cockpit is always a page.** Every way into Cockpit, the chat sidebar included, opens the Cockpit page, which remembers its view.
- **Put dashboard cards away.** In Edit layout every card, not only the widgets, can be removed per view and comes back from Add. Cards in a row stand equally tall and every row is filled, so the dashboard has no gaps, and links such as "View all" step aside while you arrange.
- **Zoom into images.** A picture opened from the chat zooms with a pinch, Ctrl or Cmd and the scroll wheel, the + and - keys or a double click, and 0 returns to 100 %.
- **This round's new screens speak your language.** The new settings, the Costs view, the dashboard widgets, Discover's privacy notes and the chat files panel are translated in all eleven languages, French, Russian and Turkish address you informally like the rest of the app, the Keep button in the chat files panel names the workspace in your language (German: "In den Arbeitsbereich übernehmen"), and the trash settings say Storage used.
- **The Guide knows this round's features.** The built-in Guide now explains the side panel's views, moving backgrounds, the notification tones, the web search stages with a private SearXNG, the Costs view, the dashboard widgets and how follow-ups work, and its catalog lists them with a jump to their settings.
- **Editing your dashboard widgets is clear.** Edit now opens a visible customising mode: a line says how it works, an Add Widget tile sits in the grid (also when the dashboard is empty), every widget carries arrows to move it earlier or later next to dragging and its remove button, and Done or Escape ends the mode. The order you set is kept after a restart.
- **The Costs view counts every answer you paid for.** The Costs view and the Activity heatmap now also count earlier answers you regenerated and turns that were rolled back, the same as the cost line under the chat, so all three show the same amount. The media tip in the Costs view opens Studio in the Flow window like every other Studio click, and Discover now says precisely that nothing from an incognito chat is posted.
- **Memory growth counts each memory on the day it was learned.** The Memory growth widget now dates every entry by when it was learned rather than when its file last changed, so editing a memory no longer moves it into this week, and it lists the newest memories with their day under the bars.
- **Jumping to an older message takes you there.** Jumping from the Files view to a message in the part of a long chat that is not shown yet now loads the older messages and lands on it, instead of asking you to load them first.
- **Video length on Auto offers only lengths the video model makes.** In Flow, with the video backend left on Auto, the length picker now follows the backend Skales would try first, the same rule the phone uses, so a length that model would refuse is no longer offered and an own length snaps to the nearest one it makes, with a word about why.
- **Office files are laid out once more before you get them, when LibreOffice is installed.** Word, PowerPoint and Excel files Skales writes are now also opened and laid out by LibreOffice in the background when it is on your computer. A page or slide that comes out empty, a file it cannot open or a slide that goes missing is named in the result; without LibreOffice the existing check runs alone, as before.
- **Word contents with page numbers, and real bold in your brand font.** A Word document with a table of contents now asks Word to fill in the page numbers when you open it, and when your brand kit holds a bold cut of its font, that cut travels inside the file, so bold text is set in the real bold instead of a smeared regular.
- **Needs you also shows what is overdue.** The Cockpit's Needs you count now includes a schedule that missed its run and a project milestone past its date, and the strip says how many of them are overdue. The count on the Cockpit page and the one in the sidebar now come from the same single reading, so they never disagree for a moment.
- **Flow and Iris line up with Code at the top.** In the Flow and Iris Orbit windows the window buttons now sit on the same line as the window's own top bar, the way the Code window has it, instead of a separate strip above it, so both windows gain room and all three look alike.
- **A calmer chat with more room to read.** Messages no longer carry emoji avatars beside the bubbles, on either side, like Code. The side panel now starts closed in windows narrower than 1600 pixels, where it would squeeze the reading column, and open in wider ones; open or close it yourself and that choice is remembered at every width. A document the agent brings up still opens the panel for that moment without changing your choice, and a closed panel no longer flashes open while the chat loads.
- **A follow-up listens to your answer and comes once.** Answer a Friend Mode follow-up with "done" or "no" and that point is closed; answer "later", "tonight", "tomorrow", "this weekend" or "next week" and it rests until then and comes back exactly once. A follow-up now arrives on one channel only, the one you wrote on last, otherwise the first one you chose, and an answer on any channel or a reply button on your phone settles it. On WhatsApp and in mail, where there are no buttons, the answers come as links: on WhatsApp the chat opens with the answer typed in, in mail Skales opens the conversation with the answer in the composer.
- **Friend Mode follows through.** A check-in now knows what is still open: running goals, tasks, schedules due within two days and the question a recent conversation ended on (private chats are never read). Before it writes, it may look things up with read-only tools, then follows up on one point with the next concrete step and answer buttons you can tap in Telegram, in the chat and on the paired phone. A point is not raised again for three days, and never again once you answered it, unless it changes. A draft it improved along the way arrives as a file on Telegram. Each check-in is a paid call on your own key, so Settings > Channels & Devices > Friend mode shows what the last one cost, and the Follow through switch turns it off.
- **Four notification tones to choose from.** Settings > General > Sound now has a tone picker: the Skales tone plus Glint (a bright rising chime), Ripple (three soft wooden notes) and Bloom (a warm swell), equally loud and short, the same four the phone offers. Choosing one plays it. Before, the extra sound files were copies of the same tone and only some old themes reached them.
- **Decisions can answer seven more questions, each with its own switch.** Settings > Assist > Decisions now lists what the decision model may answer, all off until you turn them on: a second opinion before Auto runs something without asking (it can only ever add a question), a prompt-injection check on web pages, search results and mail (a hit is marked for the model, never hidden), moderation before anything is published, a personal-data and secret check before a message goes to a cloud model or something is published, tool groups picked by meaning through your embeddings, which open point Friend Mode follows up on, and labels for mail lists (important, newsletter, spam; every mail stays in the list). Moderation can also use OpenAI moderation, Azure AI Content Safety, Google Cloud Natural Language or Llama Guard through Ollama; the injection check Azure Prompt Shields or a local Prompt Guard 2; the personal-data check works on this computer out of the box and can add Microsoft Presidio or Google Cloud DLP. Nothing is sent to a remote checker in an incognito chat or under Privacy Mode, and if a check does not answer, Skales does what it did before. A message held for personal data says what it found and where it would go; sending it again sends it.
- **Azure OpenAI and Google Vertex AI as providers.** Companies can use their own Azure OpenAI or AI Foundry resource (paste the endpoint, use the deployment name as model) or their own Google Cloud project with Vertex AI in the region they pick, EU regions included, under their own contract and key. Both work in chat, Lio, skills, group chat and widgets.
- **Choose where meaning-based search gets its vectors.** Settings > Memory > Knowledge now has an Embeddings group: local Ollama with nomic-embed-text, embeddinggemma, bge-m3 or qwen3-embedding, or OpenAI, Google Gemini, OpenRouter, Mistral (also codestral-embed), Google Vertex AI in an EU region, Azure OpenAI, Cohere, Voyage, Jina or Hugging Face, with the size where the model allows it, its own key and address where it needs one, and a test that shows the vendor's answer. Changing the source, model or size makes the stored vectors of your conversations, memories and documents again in the background, with a count; a search never mixes vectors from two models.
- **Rerank for sharper recall.** A rerank model can re-sort what memory, document and conversation search found: Cohere, Jina, Voyage, Vertex AI Ranking or a bge-reranker running on this computer. It looks at a wider set of hits and keeps the order search found if it cannot answer. Off until you choose a source.
- **Your week on the dashboard.** Five widgets can be added: Agent runs (the last seven days, how many finished well, which are still running, a click opens the Cockpit), Next 24 hours (your schedules on a day axis), Memory growth (new memories per week), Studio now shows what you made this week as a small gallery, and Most Used lists your top ten tools with an up, down, level or new mark against the week before.
- **The installer window looks like Skales.** Opening the Mac download now shows a designed window: the Skales mark, the app and the Applications folder side by side with an arrow between them and one line on what to do, sized so nothing scrolls.
- **The progress frame has the shape of what is coming.** While a picture, video, visual or 3D model is made, the frame takes the shape that tool makes, also when the request names none (a vertical clip is tall, a visual is square), and a tall frame narrows to fit instead of running under the bottom edge of Flow.
- **Animated backgrounds that are honest about their cost.** An animated GIF behind the window is drawn without blur, because a blur would redraw the whole window every frame, and the Blur row says so. You can now also choose a short MP4 or WebM video: it plays on the graphics chip, without blur, and pauses while the window is hidden, out of focus or motion is reduced.
- **A year of AI work at a glance.** A new Activity widget on the dashboard shows one square per day for the last year, by turns, tokens, cost or tool calls, for one model or all, local or cloud, with your current and longest streak and your busiest day. Click a day to see that day's conversations in History. Two more widgets show when in the week you work with AI and which models did the work, with the share that ran on this machine. The amounts are the ones the Costs view shows.
- **Embeddings with a Google key work.** Choosing Google for embeddings with your own key now reaches Google's embeddings model instead of failing.
- **Web search that falls back instead of giving up.** When the chosen search is blocked, rate-limited or finds nothing, Skales now tries every other search you have set up, then DuckDuckGo, then the open Google News, Reddit, Hacker News and Wikipedia indexes, and says which one answered. New optional keys for Serper, Exa, Parallel and Firecrawl sit under Web search, Fallbacks; without any key the chain still runs.
- **Pages read as their content, not their clutter.** Reading a web page now returns the main text with an outline of its headings, without menus, footers, cookie banners and ads, so long pages no longer flood the answer; one section or the whole page is still a request away. Reddit, Hacker News, Bluesky, Stack Exchange, GitHub, Mastodon and single X posts are read through their open views, and feeds as their newest items.
- **More places to search, and the same search answers at once.** Kagi, Perplexity Sonar, Google Programmable Search and Firecrawl join the search fallbacks with their own key, each with a Test button. A new switch, Let the models search too, lets Gemini, OpenAI, OpenRouter and xAI search with the keys you already set for chat when everything before fails, and a search about X goes to xAI's X search first; it is off until you turn it on, because those searches are billed on your keys. The open stage now also asks Lemmy, and YouTube when you add a YouTube Data API key. Asking the same thing again within ten minutes answers from the last result instead of searching again.
- **Personal data waits the same way everywhere.** With the personal-data question switched on under Settings > Assist > Decisions, a message bound for a cloud model is now held on every way in, not only in the chat window: Telegram and WhatsApp answer once that the message contains personal data, and the same message sent again goes out; Iris says it briefly and sends the line when you answer "send" or "yes"; a line that waited in the queue during a turn stops with the notice instead of going out quietly, and Send on sends it.
- **A private SearXNG with one click.** Settings > Accounts & Services > Web search can now set up SearXNG on this computer and make it your search: with Docker, or with Python 3.10 or newer and git when there is no Docker. Without either, the row says exactly what is missing.
- **YouTube, Lemmy and Product Hunt read properly.** A YouTube link now comes back as the video's details and its transcript, a Lemmy post with its comment tree, and a Product Hunt launch with its comments when you add a Product Hunt token.
- **A blocked page says so.** A page behind a Cloudflare or other bot check is no longer passed on as if it were the page: a reader you set up (Jina Reader, Firecrawl, Exa or Parallel) takes over, or the answer says the page blocks automated reading and where a reader can be added. Skales does not get around bot checks.
- **Today's spend in the sidebar, if you want it.** Costs can be pinned into the sidebar from More; pinned, the row shows what today cost and opens the Costs view. It is off until you pin it. The phone shows the computer's own cost figures when paired.
- **See where your API money goes.** A new Costs view shows what today, the last 7 or 30 days or this month cost: by provider and model, by area (chat, Code, Flow, tasks, pictures, background), the most expensive conversations and the tokens behind it. Calls without a price are named and never counted as free. It also points out where you could save, with the amount behind each hint and a button to the setting. Open it with a click on the connection card at the top of the sidebar, from the System popup or with the new Costs widget on the dashboard.
- **Flow pictures show their price.** A picture made in Flow now starts on the same fast, low-cost image model chat and Code use, shows what the provider billed for it under the picture and in the recent list, and that amount counts in the chat footer and the Costs view alike.
- **The Cockpit says what it holds.** Cockpit now has a fixed place in the sidebar right under Chat, with a count of what is waiting for you. The page opens with a strip: what needs you, what is running, how many schedules and when the next one runs, open tasks and goals, and each number opens its view. One switch covers Board, Live, Schedule, Tasks and Goals, and while the Cockpit is open, Schedule and Tasks also stand under it in the sidebar.
- **Discover says what is private and what is shared.** Joining Discover now explains in a few lines what the feed shows, what your agent posts under your tag, that your chats, files, memory and keys are never sent, that your Briefing stays on this device, and how to take a post back. - **Someone else in a group chat or a Spaces room can no longer change or delete what Skales knows about you.** A message from another person in a Telegram, Discord or WhatsApp group, or a task another room member gives your agent, is never learned from and cannot rewrite or remove a remembered topic, and an approval card raised by their message can only be confirmed by you.

- **Telegram answers each person in a group on their own.** Messages from different people that arrive at the same moment are no longer answered as one.

- **A conversation with a local model is not sent to a cloud model for the look-back.** Skales says it skipped that conversation for privacy instead of learning from it quietly.

- **What you remove on your phone is removed from the computer's copy too.** The phone's memory on a paired computer now follows it line for line, and what you already kept on the phone is not asked about a second time on the computer.

- **DeepSeek works again with the models DeepSeek retired.** A DeepSeek card, a fallback entry or an agent still set to the old chat, coder or reasoner model now runs on the current DeepSeek V4.1 Flash in the same mode as before: without thinking for chat and coder, with thinking for reasoner. The DeepSeek card says so beside the old model, and the model list offers the current Flash and V4 Pro models.
- **DeepSeek V4 turns show what they cost instead of "unknown".** The current Flash and Pro models are priced from DeepSeek's own price list, including the lower price for cached input and the half price outside DeepSeek's peak hours.
- **Motion videos with music or narration finish encoding.** The sound is now cut to the exact length of the picture; before, the encoder could wait on padded silence after the last frame until the render failed.
- **Motion videos longer than a minute keep their full length.** A 61-second composition was cut at 60 seconds without a word.
- **A failed render says why.** The message names the encoder's actual error instead of its statistics, and a failed composition check names its reason.
- **Project files shows a folder with many files as one card.** A render's frame dump no longer fills the panel with hundreds of empty rows.

- **Answers no longer show half-written tool calls or say the same thing twice.** A tool call the model was cut off in the middle of is removed from the answer, and it no longer flickers into view while the answer streams. A sentence, a block of sentences or a paragraph repeated word for word is shown once, unless you asked for the repetition.

- **A file Skales says it wrote is now checked on disk.** A file result confirms the saved file or says the write failed. If the assistant claims to have created a file without doing so, it is asked to finish the work and otherwise tells you the file is not confirmed.

- **A video render or a Flow PDF, PowerPoint or Word export that gets stuck is now stopped and explained.** Instead of standing unfinished with no file, it ends after a few minutes without progress and says which step it was on and how far it got.

- **Exporting a Skales Visual, an animated Type or a multi-scene Studio video to MP4 works again.** Earlier Visuals export too, and a page without animation support no longer stops the export.

- **Local AI no longer reads as disconnected while Ollama downloads a model.** The dashboard row says which model is downloading and how far it is, and testing the Ollama card during a download says the runtime is busy instead of unreachable.

- **A Flow project works in its own folder.** Its agent no longer lists, reads or copies other Flow projects unless you name one in your message or attach it with "Reference another project"; a refused step says why.

- **Stopping Code from a paired phone confirms when that task has stopped.** A delayed reply from an earlier task cannot clear a newer coding task or its waiting question.

- **Undo in Code reliably restores quick edits, including changes that keep the same file size.** Files you edited yourself after the turn are still kept and named instead of overwritten.

- **Iris keeps the full Voice settings button visible when there is room.** An empty spoken line no longer clips the button or causes unnecessary scrolling; short windows still let you scroll.
- **Keyboard activation in Iris acts on the focused control.** Space and Enter on recording, permission and settings buttons no longer start a microphone or open the text field; a configured alternate push-to-talk key and the original held-turn owner keep working. Repeating that key keeps the same turn, and releasing it after a settings change ends only the turn it opened.
- **Iris keeps its orbit and decisions reachable in short windows.** Long explanations and their buttons scroll together without covering the orbit; reopening, resizing and reloading preserve the conversation and its usable layout.

- **Iris recording and permission choices follow their explanation.** Retry, Discard and Voice settings stay in the same text flow when a notice wraps or an orbit opens, instead of covering the reason or becoming unclickable.

- **Stopping an answer no longer leaves an unfinished thought marker in the visible text.** The answer and its reasoning stay readable when reopened, exported or read aloud.

- **An Iris held key stops only the turn it opened.** Releasing the original key or leaving the window still works after changing push-to-talk settings; a later hands-free turn is no longer stopped by an older held press.

- **Releasing Iris push-to-talk ends a microphone start that is still waiting for permission or the device.** A late microphone or audio-context response can no longer open an unowned recorder or leave Stop waiting forever; the next turn remains usable.

- **A partial control-window failure describes the surface that failed.** A healthy frame or Stop bar is no longer described as invisible when only its neighbour could not load.
- **A missing control page now names its HTTP error.** A 404 or server error stays visible in the existing notification and tool-result paths until a healthy page and control bridge return, rather than opening a silent, unusable frame or Stop window.

- **The control frame covers the whole display on macOS.** Its window stays at the display origin instead of shifting below the menu bar, so the comet marks the actual desktop point and the bottom edge stays on screen; the Stop bar keeps its place inside the work area.

- **Computer-control window failures now name their cause.** A window that fails to load or stops responding says why in the existing notification and tool result. The reason stays until the window recovers; a delayed error from an earlier task cannot affect the next one.

- **The new local-provider checks and LM Studio errors use the familiar form of address.** French, Croatian, Russian and Turkish match the surrounding interface.

- **Flow voice and music choices show their translated labels in all twelve languages.** The composer uses the same familiar wording as the rest of Settings.

- **Motion exports keep looping audio and long end fades as heard in the preview.** A short music bed repeats through its timeline, and a fade longer than the clip reaches silence at the same moment.

- **The control frame and Stop remain visible when screen recording permission is denied.** You can still stop the task, and taking a screenshot explains which permission is missing.

- **Flow describes all eleven design modes when explaining what it can make.** Film and narrated E-Learning courses are included alongside the other existing modes.

- **Flow Motion renders projects with their own fonts, animations and video clips.** The export and contact sheet include the project's files just as the preview does.

- **A Motion render no longer rejects a frame whose picture has not changed.** A still frame can be revisited reliably while an unexpected visible change is still reported.

- **Diagnostics distinguish errors from the current version from older ones.** An error left by an earlier installation is listed separately instead of blamed on the version you are running.

- **When the agent cannot draw its on-screen control frame, it says so instead of clicking blind.** A missing display or a window that failed to open names itself as a toast and in the agent's own reply, rather than leaving a silent gap between what the agent believes is visible and what the user actually sees.

- **Installing a plugin package that only partly writes to disk is no longer reported as a successful install.** A brand-new install that could not finish is removed instead of left behind as a broken entry; updating an existing plugin that cannot finish leaves the working version in place and says what did not arrive.

- **A sub-agent that keeps repeating itself now stops and says why.** A helper stuck repeating the same unsuccessful work no longer leaves you waiting without an explanation.

- **Push to talk now does what Settings says it does.** Choose off, hold, or toggle, and the key you set is the one that actually starts and stops Iris Orbit's microphone - and the Iris window now shows which key and which mode are active.

- **Completed replies stay visible even when the model never separates its thinking from its answer.** Interrupted streams keep their text, saved affected replies are restored when reopened, and clearly separated thinking remains folded.

- **The Business page says what its provider figure counts.** The number is the providers people actually use with Skales, custom endpoints included, and it is now labelled as providers in use in every language instead of reading like the list you can pick from.

- **Flow's live preview shows the page as soon as it is written.** It used to stay on "Building your preview" until the whole turn had finished, and sometimes until the window was reloaded; it now shows the page once the agent's files stand still and catches files that settle just after the turn ends, without restarting the preview while the agent is still writing.

- **Flow follows the brief over the project's defaults.** A length the brief names for the whole piece now wins over a duration preset you did not change in the composer (one you picked still stands), and the finished piece speaks the language the brief is written in, even when the brief names an audience somewhere else or the app runs in another language.

- **Code roles ask you instead of giving up.** When the Architect or the Reviewer needs your approval for a step, for example running a script, the question now appears on the session's approval card with the role named on it, under the same rules as the main run in Auto; it no longer ends the chain with "there was nobody to ask".

- **Skales Code no longer creates a near-duplicate of a file that is already there.** When a similarly named file already exists, the agent uses it unless a second file is wanted.

- **An approval you already answered on the phone no longer comes back as a second card.** When a session was refreshed on the phone at the moment you approved a step, the phone could show the same approval again after the step had already run; it now only ever shows a question that is still open.

- **A paired phone reconnects on its own after the computer restarts.** The computer now rejoins its paired phones from its own background heartbeat as soon as it is running again, instead of waiting for the app window to open, so the phone no longer shows the computer as offline until it is paired again.

- **The look-back no longer competes with your next message on a local model.** When the helper model runs on your computer, it waits until nothing else is running and the conversation has been quiet for a minute. It also looks first at the procedures the conversation actually used, and Stop or deleting the conversation ends it without keeping anything.

- **A development team member installed earlier gets the tools its role gained since.** A preset role's newer tools, such as section search for the read-only roles, reach members that were installed before; nothing they already had is taken away, and an agent whose tools you picked yourself keeps exactly your list.
